Play online

One of you hosts and sends the room code, the other joins with it. The host is Player 1 and decides the stage and rules, and the guest is Player 2. This works on the GitHub-hosted copy of the game.

Achievements

There is one achievement for every combination of: the fighter you win with, the CPU fighter you beat, the stage, the number of stocks, and the CPU level (1 to 5). To unlock one, win a 1 vs 1 Stock match against that CPU and finish on 0% with all your stocks. Online and Timed matches don’t count. Finishing all 125 for one pairing unlocks a color: for example, all of Volt vs CPU Boulder lets Volt wear Boulder’s colors, and all of Volt vs CPU Volt unlocks Gold Volt. Pick colors on the Fighters screen. Progress is saved in this browser.

How to play

Player 1 A/D move, W jump (twice), S drop through, F attack, G special
Player 2 arrow keys, comma attack, period special
Player 3 J/L move, I jump, K drop, U attack, O special
Player 4 numpad 4/6 move, 8 jump, 5 drop, 1 attack, 2 special
Gamepads pads 1 to 4 control players 1 to 4: stick or d-pad moves, A jumps, X attacks, B special
Other keys P or Esc pauses (not online), M mutes

Hits add damage %, and the higher it is the farther fighters fly. Get knocked off the screen and you lose a stock. The small bar under each % shows when your special is ready. Teammates can’t hurt each other.
